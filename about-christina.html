My Story

Art, Passion &
Purpose

Christina has always seen the world through an artist's eyes. From a young age, creativity wasn't just a hobby — it was how she understood everything around her. That artistic instinct naturally drew her toward the beauty industry, where skin itself becomes a canvas and every treatment is an act of care.

Growing up in Brooksville, Florida, Christina was deeply rooted in her community long before she ever opened a spa. That connection to home — to the people who raised her and the county she loves — is at the heart of everything she's built. When she decided to open Cosmic Beauty Spa, she didn't look elsewhere. She brought it home.

"Bradley is the magic behind making this a reality. We built this together — and I couldn't be more proud of what we've created."
— Christina, Owner & Licensed Esthetician

Today, Christina combines her professional esthetician license, her Bioelements® certification, and her lifelong artist's eye to create skincare experiences that go far beyond a typical facial. Every client is seen as an individual. Every treatment is built around their unique skin, their real life, and their personal goals.

With big plans for expansion already in motion, this is just the beginning — and Christina is just getting started.
